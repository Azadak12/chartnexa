# Proposal

## Why
The owner requested the next UI upgrade after the guided learning journey. Explore and course details are the next step in the previously discussed discovery-to-learning flow.

## What Changes
- Visible topic and difficulty filters, full-width discovery cards with real summaries and clear actions.
- Course facts, preview invitation and enrolled next-lesson panel.
- Retry failed content and review navigation; preserve filters during debounced search.
- Authorized scope: student UI. No new backend, payments, enrollment rules, rewards or recommendations.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
None.
### Modified Capabilities
- `mobile-course-browsing`: discovery controls and course decision panels.

## Impact
Explore, discovery cards, course details and their regression tests. No migrations or new dependencies.
